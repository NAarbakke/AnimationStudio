# Burevestnik exterior and setting

The local `Burevestnik_Paper_Final_pdf.pdf` by Hecla & Kemp is the primary
exterior reference. Reviewed pages 5–8, especially Figure 2 (factory photography
and red CAD overlay), Figure 3 (exterior side profile), and Figure 4 (silhouettes).

The scene follows the visible red body, broad flattened section, blunt nose,
long pale side pods, deeper tapered rear fairing, upper horizontal tail surfaces,
and downward vertical fin. Fine seams, fasteners and paint variation are visual
interpretation; the paper does not resolve their exact construction. This is a
rendering model, not manufacturing geometry.

The ventral intake is behind the wing at the forward shoulder of the aft
fairing. The earlier forward belly box and its speculative duct were removed;
the cutaway callout now points to the aft opening. The interior flow animation
starts inside the engine without asserting an inlet duct route.

The scenery evokes Novaya Zemlya: a barren rocky coastal terrace, patchy snow,
distant mountain ridges, dark sea and cool maritime light. Landscape reference:
[NASA MODIS, Novaya Zemlya, July 27, 2009](https://modis.gsfc.nasa.gov/gallery/individual.php?db_date=2009-08-03).
The terrain is procedural, not a DEM or a surveyed reconstruction of a launch
facility. The film's source card says this explicitly.

Use `tools/pdf/inspect_pdf.py` to regenerate the paper's text and page images.
See `tools/pdf/README.md` for installation and usage. Extracted references live
in `reference/rendered/`, outside version control.
